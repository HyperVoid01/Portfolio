Synopsis

What it's about

When Armaan, a newly graduated chef, inherits a neglected neighbourhood pizzeria, he must rebuild the business from the ground up. With advice from the previous manager, players balance cooking orders and satisfying customers with cleaning the premises, eliminating rat infestations and preparing for surprise hygiene inspections. The goal: turn a rundown restaurant into a clean, efficient and highly rated pizzeria.

Project brief

The challenge

The project raises awareness of food hygiene and the consequences of inadequate sanitation in food-service establishments. Designed for players aged 13 to 18, Rat Attack presents these real-world concerns through engaging gameplay rather than a traditional educational format. Players discover how unsanitary conditions, contamination and uncontrolled pests can damage a restaurant's reputation and put customers at risk.

Core gameplay

How it plays

A PC single-player game that merges first-person cooking action with restaurant management and pest control. Players prepare meals, fulfil customer orders, keep the kitchen clean and deal with rats that threaten the restaurant.

01

Serve

Take orders from the customers queuing at the counter.

02

Cook

Make pizzas and get them out of the oven and onto the counter.

03

Clean and defend

Clear the mess and stop the rats stealing and contaminating food.

04

Upgrade

Spend your earnings on better equipment, cleaning supplies and pest control.

Features

What's in the game

First-person cooking with grab, move and rotate item handling
Customers who queue and wait for their orders
Rats that steal and eat food unless you scare them away
Dead rats and mess to clean up, so hygiene affects your reputation
Surprise hygiene inspections
Pest control bought online at RatAway.com: rat spray, mouse traps, poison and a shotgun with ammo
A five-star rating and a cash balance in rand to show how well you are doing
Systems

Under the hood

Rat AI

NavMeshAgent · Detection

Rats spot pizzas with a sphere cast, path to them, steal and eat them, and flee when the player gets close.

Order station

Customer queue

Customers line up using NavMeshAgents and the player takes the order of whoever is at the front.

Reputation

Star rating

A star rating fed by customer outcomes, with thresholds that decide how much each customer moves your score.

Item pickup

Aim and grab

Aim at an object and hold the mouse button to grab it, then move and rotate it before letting go.

Pest-control shop

In-game computer

The kitchen computer opens RatAway.com, where earnings buy rat spray, traps, poison, a shotgun and ammo.
