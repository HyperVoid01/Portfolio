Synopsis

What it's about

A futuristic city has built robots through failed experiments, and now they are hungry for power. They are coming for the city's power supply, and you are its defender. Build generators and towers, balance the energy you make against the energy you spend, and hold off every wave before the robots drain the city dry.

Project brief

The challenge

Gridlock is a futuristic, minimalist tower defense game designed around the UN Sustainable Development Goal of affordable and clean energy. Every tower has to be powered, so choosing between clean and harmful energy becomes part of the strategy, and players learn the risks of dangerous energy by playing.

Core gameplay

How it plays

A futuristic tower defense game with wave-based enemies. Towers consume energy from solar, hydro, wind and combustion sources, so players plan their power supply as carefully as their defences.

Features

What's in the game

Towers that need energy, so power production and consumption must balance
Four energy sources: solar, hydro, wind and combustion
A build stage before every round, then a timed wave of tank and walker robots
Energy income each round, shown beside the power supply's health
A futuristic, minimal look with blue and green for clean energy and red for dangerous energy
Teaches clean energy through play
