Synopsis

What it's about

Midnight. A forest road twists into the dark, lit only by your headlights, the odd street lamp and the glare of a pursuer closing in behind. An enemy car armed with guns hunts you for the entire run, and you can't fight back. Hold your nerve, drift through the bends, rack up points and reach the end alive.

Project brief

The challenge

Redline is a two-week Unity project focused on player experience: speed, tension and atmosphere. Built as a scoped-down rework of an earlier city chase game, it uses a procedural track, minimal assets and tight car handling. It is aimed at casual and arcade racing fans who enjoy quick, replayable, high-score-driven runs.

Core gameplay

How it plays

A single-player arcade racing and survival driving game. Players speed down a procedurally generated night-time forest road in an AE86, drifting for points while outrunning a gun-armed enemy car chasing them to the finish.

01

Drive

Race down a winding, procedurally generated forest road at night.

02

Drift

Slide through the bends to rack up points.

03

Evade

Stay ahead of the gun-armed car that chases you the whole way.

04

Finish

Reach the end alive and chase a higher score.

Features

What's in the game

A procedurally generated night-time forest road
Tight, drift-friendly handling in an AE86, with a handbrake
Points for drifting, shown in a live score with a speedometer
A gun-armed enemy that shoots at you for the entire run: reach the end with your car intact
Switchable camera, including a cockpit view with working gauges and a rear-view mirror
Settings for audio, mouse and stick sensitivity, steering assist and traction control
Keyboard and controller support
Moody lighting: headlights, street lamps and a pursuer's glare
