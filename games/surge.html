Synopsis

What it's about

Run across the tops of buildings, leaping from one rooftop to the next and parkouring around everything in your way. Pickups spawn randomly on every rooftop and give you buffs such as speed boosts, double jumps and phasing, so you can keep going faster or more easily. Hit an obstacle or fall off a platform and it is over, so survive for as long as you can.

Project brief

The challenge

Surge was built solo over about four months, in 2024. The goal was an endless runner with a clear skill ceiling: parkour across rooftops, where randomly generated obstacles and pickups keep every run different. The biggest lesson was to focus on the main aspects first, before working on smaller details.

My role and contribution

Solo developer: everything

I made Surge on my own over about four months.

Core gameplay

How it plays

A 3D endless runner where you traverse from building top to building top, using parkour to get around randomly generated obstacles. Power-ups found along the way help you progress, and the aim is to survive as long as possible.

01

Run

Keep moving and leap from rooftop to rooftop.

02

Dodge

Parkour around obstacles that are generated differently every run.

03

Power up

Grab random pickups for speed boosts, double jumps, phasing and more.

04

Survive

One collision or fall ends the run, so survive as long as you can.

Features

What's in the game

3D rooftop parkour endless runner
Randomly generated obstacles and pickups at each rooftop's spawn points
Pickups with buffs: speed boosts, double jumps, phasing and more
A run that ends if you hit an obstacle or fall off a platform
A live score, a completed-levels counter and a compass
A leaderboard of players, levels completed and score
Simple controls: A and D to strafe, P to use a pickup
