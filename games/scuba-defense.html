Synopsis

What it's about

Deep beneath the ocean, a lone scuba diver guards a vital central base from waves of hostile sea creatures emerging from caves and swarming along winding paths. Armed with a pickaxe, the diver digs into the seabed to harvest ore, then converts it into towers. Every wave is tougher, and every dig reshapes the terrain, forcing players to balance mining, building and survival.

Project brief

The challenge

Scuba Defense is an entertainment game focused on player experience and procedural generation. Every playthrough features a unique seabed, built with 3D noise and Marching Cubes, with spline-generated enemy paths and randomly placed ore. It targets casual to mid-core strategy and tower defense players who enjoy replayability and tactical planning.

Core gameplay

How it plays

A single-player, first-person tower defense game. Players mine resources from procedurally generated seabed terrain, spend them on towers, and place them along winding enemy paths to survive escalating waves.

01

Dig

Swing the pickaxe to carve into the seabed. Every dig reshapes the terrain.

02

Mine

Harvest randomly placed ore from the ground.

03

Build

Spend ore on towers and place them along the winding enemy paths.

04

Survive

Hold the base against each tougher wave of sea creatures.

Features

What's in the game

A unique seabed on every playthrough
Enemy paths generated from splines
Five resources to find and mine: iron, copper, pearls, wood and gold
Dig with the pickaxe (left mouse button) to mine and reshape the terrain
A tower shop with the Bubble Gun, Harpoon and Lazer Gun, each with its own resource cost
Escalating waves of creatures from the caves, with a countdown to the next wave
Systems

Under the hood

Procedural seabed

3D noise · Marching Cubes

Terrain built from 3D noise and the Marching Cubes algorithm, so every game has a different seabed you can dig into.

Enemy paths

Splines

Winding paths generated from splines that creatures follow from the caves to your base.

Resources and towers

Mining · Economy

Iron, copper, pearls, wood and gold pay for the Bubble Gun, Harpoon and Lazer Gun in the tower shop, so digging and defending compete for your time.

Wave system

Spawning · Difficulty

Waves spawn from the caves and grow tougher each round.
